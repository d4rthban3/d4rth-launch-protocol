The collection

The main protocol is USDC · Protocol Hub. The hub coin pairs with USDC, SOL and the central Star: the main pool is USDC, 10% locks to Star, and 10% locks to SOL. Other planets lock a chunk of their supply to the Protocol Hub.

USDT is an optional extra boost: add a secondary pool paired to USDT and the same planets pull closer to the center core.
