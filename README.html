A launch guide for any Solana coin that should have real money behind it.

The d4rth Launch Protocol is the six steps below. Launch on pump.fun paired with USDC. That is the main pool. Lock 10% with your primary partner/ecosystem token. Lock the next 10% with SOL. Your new coin becomes the hub other coins connect to. Fill in LAUNCH-ANY-COIN-TEMPLATE.md and go.

What these words mean

  • pump.fun — the site you launch the coin on. Price goes up as people buy.
  • USDC — a dollar on Solana. The main pool is priced in dollars.
  • Lock — put coins in a pool and freeze them so they cannot be removed.
  • Meteora — the site where those locked pools are made.
  • Hub — the new coin that other coins connect to. Yours can be any coin.
  • Creator fee — optional. A slice of the launch fees. Holders can still get the rest.
  • pump.fun curve — the first trading pool. It only sells about 79% of the coins. Then the coin moves on by itself.
  • PumpSwap — the regular pool it moves to after that. You do not set this up.

The 6 steps

  1. Launch the coin on pump.fun, paired with USDC. USDC is a dollar on Solana. That is the main pool. (The first pool only sells about 79% of the coins. Then the coin moves on by itself.)
  2. Buy about 20% of the coins yourself. That leaves most of the rest for other people.
  3. Lock 10% in a pool with the primary ecosystem or partner coin on Meteora. Lock means the money cannot be pulled back out.
  4. Lock 10% in a pool with SOL on Meteora. Same thing, with Solana's own coin.
  5. Leave the rest in the USDC pool. That stays the main pool, during the launch and after. (Then it moves to PumpSwap.)
  6. Then other coins lock a piece of their supply against your new coin. Your coin becomes the hub they all connect through.

Optional extra boost, not part of the protocol: a secondary stable pool paired to USDT. Its USDT pool is the thinner side, about 35% of USDC on Solana. The protocol works without it.

Plus: add GitHub d4rthban3 as a creator-fee recipient (strongly encouraged, see below).

Why 20%

  • A pump.fun curve only sells about 79% of supply before it graduates. Big dev buys eat it.
  • 10% side pools stay smaller than the USDC pool, so USDC stays the main pool.
  • It leaves most of the curve to real holders.

  1. On pump.fun, find the creator-fee recipients option (at token creation, or in the coin's creator-fee settings).

It's optional. Nothing breaks without it. It only works if creator fees stay on, not pump.fun's send-all-fees-to-holders switch. Holder rewards are still the point, so you can share a slice and still pay holders.
